About

Welcome to flavoseason — where culinary excellence, creative events, and fine art come together under one vision.

I'm Charbel, a passionate chef with a deep love for creating unforgettable dining experiences. Whether it's an intimate private dinner, a grand celebration, or a commissioned painting that captures a moment in time, I bring the same dedication and artistry to everything I do.

My journey began in the kitchen, where I discovered that cooking is much more than preparing food — it's storytelling through flavors. That same creative spirit extends to my event planning and my canvas, where colors and textures tell their own stories.
